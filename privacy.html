Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 25, 2026

Who this app is for

"YouTube Studio (personal use)" is a private tool used only by its developer, Carlos, to manage his own YouTube channel. No other user is invited to use it.

What data the app accesses

When the developer signs in with his Google account, the app uses YouTube API Services to access data from his own channel only: video uploads and metadata, comments on his videos, and the channel's analytics and performance metrics.

How the data is used

The data is used only to publish videos to the channel and to show the developer how his own videos are performing, so he can improve future videos. It is not used for advertising, is not sold, and is not shared with any third party.

Where the data is stored

Data and access tokens are stored on the developer's own computer. Nothing is published or exposed to other people.

Google API Services User Data Policy

The app's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

YouTube API Services

By using this app, the developer agrees to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's privacy practices are described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
